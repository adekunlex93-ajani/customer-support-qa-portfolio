# Customer Support Case Study

## Overview

This case study demonstrates my approach to handling a technical customer support issue from the initial customer report through investigation, communication, resolution, and follow-up.

The example is based on the type of software and website support work I performed while working at Senior Internet. Client and company-specific information has been excluded for confidentiality.

---

## Scenario

A client reported an issue with a website feature that was not behaving as expected.

The customer needed assistance understanding what was happening and expected the issue to be investigated and resolved as quickly as possible.

---

## My Approach

### 1. Understand the Issue

I first reviewed the customer's message carefully to understand:

* What the customer was experiencing
* Which part of the website or software was affected
* When the issue occurred
* Whether the issue could be reproduced

I asked for additional information when necessary to make sure I understood the problem correctly.

### 2. Investigate

I attempted to reproduce the reported issue and checked the affected website or software functionality.

During the investigation, I checked:

* The affected page or feature
* Expected versus actual behaviour
* Whether the issue occurred consistently
* Whether other parts of the website were affected
* Basic browser and user-interface behaviour

### 3. Document the Issue

When I identified a problem, I documented the relevant information so that the internal team could understand and investigate it.

The information included:

* Issue description
* Steps to reproduce
* Expected behaviour
* Actual behaviour
* Affected page or feature
* Supporting screenshots or evidence when available

### 4. Communicate With the Customer

I provided the customer with a clear response explaining the status of the issue.

If the issue required further investigation by another team, I communicated that it had been escalated and provided an update rather than leaving the customer without information.

### 5. Follow Up

After the issue was investigated or a fix was provided, I checked the affected functionality again to confirm whether the problem had been resolved.

Where appropriate, I updated the customer and closed the issue after confirming resolution.

---

## Example Support Workflow

```text
Customer reports issue
        ↓
Understand the problem
        ↓
Investigate / reproduce
        ↓
Document findings
        ↓
Resolve or escalate
        ↓
Update customer
        ↓
Retest after fix
        ↓
Confirm resolution
        ↓
Close ticket
```

## Key Takeaway

This experience taught me the importance of combining good customer communication with structured technical investigation. A support issue is not simply about providing an answer; it is also about understanding the problem, identifying the cause or next action, keeping the customer informed, and confirming that the issue has been resolved.
